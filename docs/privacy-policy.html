Farah Maritime English and Arabic – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

Farah Maritime English and Arabic is an offline educational application for learning basic maritime English and Arabic terminology.

Information the app handles

The app does not ask you to create an account and does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information with the app developer. Learning progress, quiz scores, and saved vocabulary are stored locally on your device using the app's local storage.

Voice pronunciation

When you use the pronunciation feature, the selected English word is passed to the Android text-to-speech service available on your device. The service may be provided by Google or another provider selected in your Android settings. Its handling of data is governed by that provider's own privacy terms and settings. The app itself does not send the word to its own server.

Network access and third parties

The app is designed to work offline. It does not include advertising, analytics, account registration, or third-party advertising SDKs.

Data retention and deletion

Local learning data remains on your device. You can clear the app's storage in Android settings or uninstall the app to remove the locally stored app data.

Children

The app is a general educational tool and does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if the app's features or data practices change. The latest version will be published at this page.

Contact

For questions about this policy, contact the developer through the contact details shown on the app's Google Play listing.
