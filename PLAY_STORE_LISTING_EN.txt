App name: Farah Maritime English and Arabic

Short description:
Learn ship parts and maritime terms in English and Arabic with offline lessons and quizzes.

Full description:
Farah Maritime English and Arabic is a beginner-friendly learning app for people interested in ships and maritime work. It introduces basic maritime English vocabulary with Arabic meanings in a simple bilingual format.

Features:
• Bilingual lessons about ship parts, equipment, and maritime terminology.
• English terms with Arabic meanings.
• Short quizzes to review lessons and check learning progress.
• Save useful vocabulary and keep learning progress on your device.
• English word pronunciation through the Android text-to-speech service available on your device.
• Designed to work offline after installation.

This is an educational app for beginners. It is not a substitute for approved maritime training, onboard safety procedures, or official operational instructions.

Suggested category: Education
Suggested audience: Beginners, maritime students, and people interested in ship terminology.
